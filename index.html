Biography

Héctor Jiménez is a Spanish violist whose work is centered on contemporary music. Based in Leipzig, he studies at the Hochschule für Musik und Theater «Felix Mendelssohn Bartholdy» in the class of Prof. Sara Kim, having begun his undergraduate studies there with Prof. Pauline Sachse.

He trained in Madrid at the Conservatorio Profesional de Música «Victoria de los Ángeles» with Raquel Tavira, and took private lessons with Jaime Huertas, violist of the Cuarteto Leonor. He played principal viola with the Youth Camerata of Madrid and the Youth Orchestra of Madrid (JORCAM). His early successes include a first prize at the National Chamber Music Competition «Ciudad de Ávila» (2021) and, at the national final of Jugend musiziert, a second prize in viola solo and a third in early music (2022), followed by a third prize in new music (2023).

His interest in contemporary music took shape in composition and music theory lessons with the Madrid-based composer José Luis Torá, and he also takes part in Fabien Lévy’s composition seminar in Leipzig. His repertoire includes works by Morton Feldman, Salvatore Sciarrino, Mark Andre, Liza Lim and George Benjamin, as well as frequent premieres of pieces by younger composers.

He has developed this repertoire at the Lucerne Festival Academy, the Essen Now Festival with Studio Musikfabrik, the Messiaen-Tage with Ensemble Écoute and the World New Music Days with Concrète Lab Ensemble, and in masterclasses with Garth Knox, Stephen Upshaw, Tatjana Masurenko, Rachel Koblyakov and Dejana Sekulić, among others.

He also plays regularly with the Philharmonie Leipzig in a wide range of repertoire and styles, from film music and symphonic gothic rock concerts to the traditional symphonic repertoire, and has performed with it at venues such as the Berliner Philharmonie, Tonhalle Zürich, Historische Stadthalle Wuppertal, Stadtcasino Basel and the Isarphilharmonie. He has also performed in France, Switzerland, Austria, Germany, Portugal, Poland and Spain.
